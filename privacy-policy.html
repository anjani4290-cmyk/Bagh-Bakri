Bagh Bakri – Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026

Bagh Bakri is a traditional strategy board game developed by Anjani Kumar Rai.

Information We Collect

The current version of Bagh Bakri does not require an account or sign-in and does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, or other personal information.

Game Data

Basic game data may be stored locally on your device so that the game can function. This information is not intentionally transmitted to a personal database or server by the current version of the app.

Advertising and Analytics

The current version of Bagh Bakri does not display advertisements and does not intentionally use third-party advertising or analytics services. If advertising or third-party services are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Children's Privacy

Bagh Bakri is a family-friendly board game. The current version does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Deletion

Locally stored game data can generally be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app from your device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features, data practices, advertising, or third-party services change. The updated policy will be published at this URL.
