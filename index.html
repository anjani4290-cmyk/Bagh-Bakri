How to Play

  1. Each player starts with 16 pieces (goti): Red across the top, Green across the bottom.
  2. Players take turns moving one piece at a time. Red moves first unless chosen otherwise.
  3. A normal move slides a piece one step along a drawn line to an empty point.
  4. A piece may move along any line touching it — straight or diagonal — but only where a line is actually drawn.
  5. Capture an opponent's piece by jumping over it in a straight line to an empty point directly beyond it.
  6. After a capture, the same piece keeps jumping if another capture is available — you can't switch pieces mid-chain.
  7. The centre point starts empty and is a useful crossing point for both sides.
  8. The first player to capture all 16 of the opponent's pieces wins. A player with no legal move at all ends the game in a draw.
  9. Playing vs Computer: pick your colour and the computer plays the other side automatically, always following the same rules (including forced capture chains).
